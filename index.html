Canvas guide

Frame pencil
Draw a closed outline to shape the video.
Color pencil
Sketch around the video. Adjust stroke width and pick a preset or custom color.
Eraser
Erase video, frame edges, and pencil marks. Adjust brush size in the popover.
Shape stamps
Tap a rectangle to create a widescreen, square, or classic frame.
Shapes
Draw rectangles, ellipses, triangles, and stars. Select a shape to move or resize it, or use Color fill to recolor it.
Images
Choose a painted background or upload your own. Adjust the fit and opacity, then draw over the canvas.
Video palette
Hover, focus, or tap the film icon to switch clips or add your own videos.
Player controls
Play, seek, change volume, and open playback settings for speed, captions, tracks, quality, and device playback.
Keyboard
Space plays or pauses, M mutes, F toggles fullscreen, and the arrow keys seek.
